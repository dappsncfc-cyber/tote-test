Craftsmanship & Materials

Why Our Prints Outlast the Rest

We never print on flimsy throwaway promotional blanks. Everything is built to endure everyday utility.

🧵

Reinforced Cross-Stitch Handles

Tote bag handles feature reinforced 'X-box' stitched webbing rated for 18kg+ loads, so heavy laptops and produce won't pull the seams apart.

💧

Zero-Plastic Water Inks

Unlike cheap plastisol transfers that crack after 2 washes, we screen print with water-based discharge inks that dye into cotton fibres for a silky, uncrackable feel.

👕

Premium Heavyweight Cotton

Heavyweight 100% fair-wage combed cotton built for durability and long-lasting wear. Pre-shrunk to retain shape wash after wash.
